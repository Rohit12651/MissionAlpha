What you decide, and what you never have to

Three gates.
No surprises.

the team asks; it never assumes

  1. 01

    The CEO says proceed, defer or kill

    Before any planning, a CEO agent judges the brief and names a cheaper option. If it says defer, you are asked a plain yes or no.

  2. 02

    You answer the Manager's questions

    Every open question arrives on one card, one answer each. Nothing is planned on a guess.

  3. 03

    You approve the ship

    You see every file each worker changed. Approve, and it lands on its own branch; send it back with a note, and the team tries again.

  4. ✓

    What workers can never touch

    Workers run inside a sandbox, and the runtime's own code is off-limits to them entirely. They change your project, not the thing that runs them.
